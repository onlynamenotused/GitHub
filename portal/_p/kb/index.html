Mimaki

Links go to Mimaki Australia. Each model has its operation manuals page and a download page with printer drivers, utilities and the RasterLink device profiles for your ink set. Firmware for the JV/CJV300 and 150 series is updated through Mimaki's updater; we can apply it for you at your next service.

Print widths and speeds are the manufacturer's published figures; speeds are the fastest mode and real-world output is lower. The default ink set is the ink line and head layout the machine is shipped with, from Mimaki's installation guides; using it needs no change to the ink system. Any other ink set needs the ink paths changed by a technician. No Mimaki printer has Wi-Fi, and RasterLink runs on Windows only, so Mac users need a Windows PC for the RIP.

Mutoh

Mutoh has no Australian support site, so these link to its European support portal for firmware, user guides and technical bulletins.

Brother GTX

Direct-to-garment printers. Use the printer driver and firmware versions together, as Brother recommends.

Epilog

Epilog has no Australian support site; its US support site holds the owner's manual for every laser, current and discontinued, and the print driver and firmware for each model.

Cleaning & maintenance

Daily and weekly user maintenance. Washing liquids, swabs, wipes and other consumables are in the Parts Catalogue.

Match the liquid to your ink. Washing liquids are made for one ink chemistry. The wrong one can block or damage print heads, so check your ink type first, or ask us. Never use household cleaners, cotton buds or tissues on print heads, caps or wipers.

Office lasers

Brother and Kyocera office printers. Tap a model for its manufacturer downloads. Toner, drums and belts are in the Parts Catalogue.

Test prints

Reference files for checking colour, banding, white and clear layers, contour cutting and engraving after a service, an ink change or a new profile. Download, open in your RIP and print at 100% scale.

Downloads come from the Highway Digital OneDrive. The larger files (up to 178 MB) can take a few minutes. You can also browse every public test file in one folder. For files marked On request, email hello@highwaydigital.com.au with the file name and we'll send you a download link.
