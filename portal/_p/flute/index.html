Check a recording

Record yourself playing the selected tune with your phone's voice recorder or camera, then choose the file here. Each note gets marked under the tune: green = right note, orange = close (a semitone off, wrong octave, or out of tune), red = wrong or missed. A ↑ or ↓ means the right note but sharp or flat.

Type in your own notes

Use note names from any sheet or video, separated by spaces. Add a length after a colon if you like: E:2 = two beats, G:.5 = half a beat. Octave numbers are optional (E5 = the higher E).
