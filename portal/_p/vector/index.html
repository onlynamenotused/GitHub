Getting the best result
  • Use the largest, sharpest copy of the artwork you have.
  • Set Colours to the number of colours in the logo, plus one for the background.
  • Photos and soft gradients don't trace cleanly; contact us if you need artwork redrawn.
  • The SVG opens in Illustrator, CorelDRAW, Inkscape, Affinity and most RIP / cutting software.
