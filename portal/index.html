About Highway Digital

Highway Digital is a Perth-based equipment service business. We install, service and repair wide-format printers, print-and-cut machines, laser engravers and fabric printers, specialising in Mimaki, Mutoh, Brother and Epilog. We also support customers across regional WA and interstate, and provide IT services for homes and small businesses.

What's in the Service Desk

  • My machines: every machine on your account, with serial numbers, service history and when the next service is due.
  • Add a machine: scan its serial plate with your phone and set its service schedule.
  • Book a service or log a job: choose a free time from our calendar, or log a job and we'll call you to arrange it.
  • Manuals and downloads: manuals, drivers and firmware links for your models, plus your machine's saved parameter and log files.
  • Instant service quotes (in development): press Quote me on any machine to get a quote for its service and PM parts, emailed to you straight away. Choose an annual or 6-month service, add or remove parts, and tick more machines to service them all on one callout.
  • Invoices and estimates: see what's outstanding and pay or accept online.
  • Knowledge Base and Parts Catalogue: fixes for common faults, and parts and consumables with pricing.
  • Vector Converter Beta: turn logos and images into vector files, add cut lines, convert spot colors to print white, add crop marks, and download print-ready PDF, EPS or DXF files.
  • Fleet reports for dealers and vendor partners: job details, parts used, trends and machine stats.

Customers

Tick Knowledge Base to give that customer's logins the Knowledge Base, tick Vector Converter to give them the image-to-vector and print & cut tool, tick Flute Practice to give them the flute practice player (off for every customer unless ticked), tick Apps only for a login that should only see its apps (no machines or booking), and pick the Parts Catalogue brands (Mimaki, Mutoh, Brother, Epilog, Others) that customer can see, with prices. Untick every brand to remove catalogue access. New customers start with the Knowledge Base and Vector Converter unticked and only Others ticked in the catalogue; tick the brands of the machines they own. Changes save straight away. Sub-contractor logins always get the Knowledge Base and never the catalogue. Vector exports counts every file that customer's logins have downloaded from the Vector Converter (hover the number for the file types and last date).
